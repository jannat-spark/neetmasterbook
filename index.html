The operating loop

Plan. Execute.
Measure. Correct.

Four moves, repeated every week. The loop is the product.

  1. 01

    Plan

    Know exactly what comes next. Year → sprint → week → today.

  2. 02

    Execute

    Finish today's work. Three tasks, measurable, closed every night.

  3. 03

    Measure

    Mocks and PYQs tell the truth. Log every score, every mistake.

  4. 04

    Correct

    Fix the mistake that repeats. Errors become revision tasks.

Not a one-time plan. A loop you run until the exam.
